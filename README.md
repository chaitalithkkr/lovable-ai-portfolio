# ai-portfolio

A collection of AI-powered product concepts exploring career clarity and self-discovery, built during Lovable's SheBuilds hackathon for International Women's Day.

Both projects come back to the same theme: **helping people find clarity**.

---

## Projects

### 1. Clearline — Career Clarity & Interview Prep
> Lovable · Prompt Engineering · Product Design

A career clarity concept for people with nonlinear backgrounds who want to better understand role fit, connect their past experiences into a stronger story, and prepare for interviews with more intention.

**Core ideas:**
- Help users identify transferable strengths across nonlinear career paths
- Surface patterns between past experience and target roles
- Support more confident, structured interview preparation

**Future vision:** backend persistence, stronger recommendation systems, and a community layer connecting people through similar roles, experience levels, and career journeys.

🔗 [Try Clearline](https://lnkd.in/gxT9_BvC)

---

### 2. Mirror Mode — Personal Clarity & Self-Discovery
> Lovable · Prompt Engineering · Product Design

A self-discovery concept exploring how hard it can be to figure out who you really are — what you resonate with, what feels aligned with your personality, interests, and vibe.

**Core ideas:**
- Help users explore their identity, values, and aesthetic sensibilities
- Surface patterns in what genuinely resonates vs. what doesn't
- Make self-discovery feel fun, low-pressure, and personal

**Future vision:** backend persistence and a community layer connecting people through shared interests, vibes, and personality patterns.

🔗 [Try Mirror Mode](https://lnkd.in/gpvHmuwR)

## The Common Thread

| Project | Type of Clarity |
|---|---|
| Clearline | Professional clarity — role fit, career story, interview confidence |
| Mirror Mode | Personal clarity — identity, values, resonance |

Clearline explores who you are professionally. Mirror Mode explores who you are personally. Both start with the same belief: clarity is the foundation for confident decision-making.
